Research Study Questionnaire

基於功能性近紅外線光譜技術(fNIRS)探討顯示屏幕設備使用者慢性非特異性頸痛與執行功能的病例對照研究

EXECUTIVE FUNCTION IN DISPLAY SCREEN EQUIPMENT USERS WITH CHRONIC NON-SPECIFIC NECK PAIN: A CASE-CONTROL fNIRS STUDY

香港理工大學誠邀閣下參與由香港理工大學康復治療科學系手法物理治療理學碩士課程的一組學生進行的上述研究項目。此項研究的指導教授為香港理工大學康復治療科學系曾敏霞教授及鄺偉恒教授。此項目已由香港理工大學研究倫理審查委員會批准 (參考編號: HSEARS20260828006)。本研究運用非侵入性且無輻射之光源測繪技術，於執行功能測試期間，調查並比較顯示屏幕設備使用者中，患有機械性慢性非特異性頸痛者與健康對照組之執行功能表現及前額葉皮層血氧變化。

個人資料的保密

您在此項目中提供的資料屬於研究數據。任何可以識別您身份的研究數據均屬個人資料。個人資料不包括已移除身份識別資訊的數據（匿名數據）。我們將盡可能在研究中減少使用您的個人資料。研究人員及研究團隊及其指導教授（包括香港理工大學的曾敏霞教授及鄺偉恒教授）將因應研究需要存取個人資料和研究數據。理工大學的授權人員或會獲准查閱資料，以進行研究監察及審計。

資料保存期限

所有關於您的資料將會保密，僅通過研究者所知的代碼進行識別，並進行文件加密。所收集的信息將在研究完成後保留 3 年。香港理工大學將採取合適的預防措施以防止您提供的資料遺失、遭盜用、未經授權的存取或銷毁。

You are invited to participate in the above project conducted by a group of students studying at MSc in Manipulative Physiotherapy at The Department of Rehabilitation Sciences of The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU) under the supervision of Prof. Sharon Tsang, Associate Professor and Prof. Patrick Kwong, Assistant Professor of The Department of Rehabilitation Sciences of PolyU. The project has been approved by the PolyU Institutional Review Board (PolyU IRB) (or its Delegate) (Reference Number: HSEARS20260828006). The aim of this project is to investigate and compare executive function performance and prefrontal cortex oxygenation using non-invasive and radiation-free light source mapping during executive function tasks between Display Screen Equipment (DSE) users with chronic non-specific neck pain and healthy controls.

Confidentiality of Personal Data

The information you provide as part of the project is the research data. Any research data from which you can be identified is known as personal data. Personal data does not include data where the identity has been removed (anonymous data). We will minimize our use of personal data in the study as much as possible. The researcher and his supervisors (including Prof. Sharon Tsang and Prof. Patrick Kwong of PolyU) will have access to personal data and research data for the purposes of the study. Responsible members of PolyU may be given access for monitoring and/or audit of the research.

Data Retention

All information related to you will remain confidential and will be identifiable by codes only known to the researchers with file encryption. The information collected will be kept for 3 years after project completion. The Hong Kong Polytechnic University takes reasonable precautions to prevent the loss, misappropriation, unauthorized access or destruction of the information you provide.
